# Last light — score

One musical idea carries the whole game: **the chick's theme**, five tender, rising
notes. It is heard small at the start (celesta, harp), it gets lost when the parents
leave, it is missing while the chick is alone, it comes back hummed in the storm, and it
breaks into the major key at the end. Play itself stays almost without music: the wind
and the colony are enough. The music comes mostly with the cinematics, so that each one
counts.

Extended chamber orchestra: strings, solo cello, oboe, horn, harp, celesta, felt piano,
timpani, wordless choir. Home key D minor / D major. Slow tempo (56–72 BPM).

The cues are generated with ElevenLabs Music (`music_v2_5`, instrumental) and fitted to
the length of the shots. The player cross-fades between cues and lowers the music during
play.
