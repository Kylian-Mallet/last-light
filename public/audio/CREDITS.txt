Emperor penguin chick calls (chick_zs_*.m4a) and the crèche chatter loop (loop_creche.m4a)
  Isolated from a "Zapping Sauvage" clip (France Télévisions) found on YouTube.
  High-passed above 1.15-1.2 kHz to strip the voice-over and music, denoised, trimmed;
  the loop joins the stretches between narration lines.
